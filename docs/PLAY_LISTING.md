## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Rooster is a solar-aware alarm clock that ties your wake-up time to the
sun, not just a number on a dial. Set an alarm for "30 minutes before
sunrise" or "civil dusk" and Rooster handles the math — every day, year
round, wherever you are.

Built for people who want a calmer, more natural rhythm to their days
without giving up modern alarm features.

═══ Why Rooster ═══

🌅  Solar event scheduling
    Set alarms relative to sunrise, sunset, civil/nautical/astronomical
    twilight, or solar noon. Times update automatically as the seasons
    shift.

🔔  Themed alarm tones for each twilight stage
    Rooster crow at sunrise. Morning birds at civil dawn. Ocean waves at
    nautical dawn. Wind chimes at noon. Church bells at sunset. Cricket
    song, owl hoot, gentle wind for the dusk stages. All sourced from
    Wikimedia Commons with full attribution in the app's Credits screen.

📆  Per-alarm repeat days, snooze, vibration, gradual volume
    Everything you'd expect from a serious alarm clock — labels, repeat
    days, custom snooze, optional vibration, optional gradual volume
    ramp-up.

🎨  Glass-morphic Material 3 design
    Sunrise / sunset gradient backgrounds, dynamic colors on Android 12+,
    light and dark themes.

📐  Solar ring home-screen widget
    See the day's solar arc and your next alarm at a glance.

═══ Privacy and ethics ═══

🔒  No ads. No analytics. No tracking SDKs.
    Rooster contains zero third-party tracking code. No Firebase, no
    crash reporting, no attribution networks. The only external library
    that touches your data is Google Play Services Location, which the
    Android system uses to give the app your coordinates.

📍  Location stays on your device.
    Sunrise, sunset, and twilight times are computed on-device with a
    built-in solar calculator. Your coordinates are never transmitted to
    any server controlled by the developer.

💸  One-time purchase. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
    You pay once on the Play Store and you're done. The price is the
    price.

📜  Open source under GPL-3.0.
    Source code, issue tracker, and contribution guide live at
    https://github.com/thdelmas/Rooster. Privacy policy at
    https://github.com/thdelmas/Rooster/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md.

═══ Permissions, briefly ═══

• Location — to compute solar times for your specific place
• Exact alarm — so the alarm fires on time, not "around" the time
• Notifications — so the alarm can ring when the screen is off
• Wake lock & full-screen intent — to wake you up reliably

Rooster doesn't ask for contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, SMS, or
any other personal data.

═══ Compatibility ═══

Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and newer.

═══ Contact ═══

contact@theophile.world
```

(approx. 2,700 chars)
